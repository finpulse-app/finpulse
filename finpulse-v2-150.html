Fixed vs. Variable
Why we split your spending this way, tap to learn more
▼
Fixed
The same amount every month. Rent, subscriptions, loan payments. Hard to change quickly, this is your floor.
Variable
Changes based on your choices. Food, shopping, fun. This is where you have the most control to save.
Why it matters: most financial guidance says keeping fixed costs under 50-60% of income leaves you room to breathe. If a month gets tight, variable spending is the first place to look, not your rent.
